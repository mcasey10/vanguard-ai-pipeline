Read-only ST gains · LT gains · Losses harvested · Est. total tax · Effective tax rate

Read-only Optimization scoring: tax impact vs. FIFO baseline · drift improvement · account priority compliance
Read-only Plain-language tradeoff summary (e.g., "Lowest tax impact but leaves bonds slightly underweight")

Fund Selection shows one configuration at a time · Scenario Analysis shows up to 3 side by side
Portfolio allocation detail (before/after/target bar chart) lives here, not in Fund Selection
Optimization scoring detail lives here, not in Fund Selection
No mode toggle here — scenarios are mode-agnostic once saved
